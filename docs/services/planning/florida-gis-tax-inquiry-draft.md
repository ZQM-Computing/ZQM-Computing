Any professional environmental, surveying, engineering, geological or permitting opinion remains outside this provider's independent service unless separately authorized by law. Maps are not sold to the general public or reused as standardized reports.

**Question:** Do these custom electronic deliverables constitute nontaxable personal/professional service or non-taxable information delivered as electronic images under Rule 12A-1.062, or is any component subject to Florida sales/use tax?

### Offer B — single-client desktop mapped-indicator screening

Provider compiles authorized parcel/land-cover/flood/wetland indicator GIS datasets, maps source overlap and missingness and writes a customer-specific report. The service does not establish surveyed boundaries or regulatory wetland determinations. A client reviewing professional must decide regulatory significance. Reports are prepared exclusively for that customer and are not part of a resold data publication.

**Question:** Is this custom research and digitally delivered map/report excluded from taxable information services under Rule 12A-1.062(3)(b) and (5)? Which facts would change the result?
