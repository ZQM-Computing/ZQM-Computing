ZQM Computing helps engineering, environmental, infrastructure and GIS teams investigate their recurring spatial data workflows and plan auditable improvements. Open-source reference examples are freely accessible; any paid engagement is scoped and contracted separately.

## Explore services

- [GIS workflow automation and diagnostic](services/gis-workflow-automation.md) — assess and improve a repeating geospatial process.
- [PostGIS consulting and migration discovery](services/postgis-consulting.md) — evaluate spatial query performance, database quality and migration risks.
- [Preliminary environmental GIS screening](services/environmental-gis-screening.md) — map published environmental indicators with documented source limitations.

## How engagement works

Start with a fit discussion, agree authorized inputs and a written scope, validate output against explicit quality requirements, and review the findings before any implementation decision.
