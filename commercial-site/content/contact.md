ZQM Computing reviews prospective GIS, spatial database, and preliminary desktop screening engagements individually. This is an **initial fit conversation**, not an automated order, a confirmed booking, or a professional regulatory determination.

## What to include in an inquiry

To assess whether a defined engagement is appropriate, summarize:

1. **General objective:** the decision or operational workflow you need to improve.
2. **Work category:** GIS workflow automation, PostGIS, data migration, or preliminary environmental screening.
3. **General geography and data sources:** jurisdiction or region, and software/data formats; do not include private parcel-owner details or precise sensitive locations.
4. **Expected deliverable:** for example a QA report, automated processing workflow, database diagnostic, or preliminary constraints map.
5. **Project timing:** desired decision date or general procurement window.
6. **Constraints:** accessibility, licensing, procurement, or privacy requirements relevant to the proposed scope.

**Do not send passwords, access tokens, confidential data exports, protected personal information, or proprietary client datasets through an initial email.** Data sharing, authorization, retention and scope are agreed separately.

## What happens after an inquiry?

If the request appears to fit, the next step is to confirm a brief scoping discussion. A written proposal, deliverables, source-data rights, evaluation standards, contracting identity and commercial terms must be agreed before work begins. No specific response time or acceptance is guaranteed.
