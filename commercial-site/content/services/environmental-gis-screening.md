A preliminary desktop screen organizes spatial indicators that may be relevant to early planning. It does not establish field conditions or substitute for professional determinations.

## What can parcel-level GIS screening identify?

Depending on authorized inputs and local data availability, a screen may show published flood-hazard zones, wetland inventory, elevation, land-cover and other available constraints alongside an area of interest. Each layer requires source, date, scale, resolution and licensing review.

## What is included in an assessment packet?

Proposed outputs may include annotated maps, data-source inventories, QA findings, uncertainty statements and recommended follow-up verification steps. Jurisdiction-specific availability must be checked; worldwide parcel coverage is not guaranteed.

## What are the limitations?

Published wetlands and flood maps are screening indicators, not jurisdictional wetland delineations, certified surveys, flood determinations or legal access statements. The work does **not** constitute an environmental site assessment, professional engineering opinion or regulatory approval.
