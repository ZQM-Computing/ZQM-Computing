Repetitive GIS workflows become more reliable when inputs, coordinate systems, validation rules, transformations, outputs and failure recovery are documented and tested.

## What does a GIS workflow diagnostic deliver?

A proposed diagnostic examines one recurring workflow, representative inputs and a target output. The findings identify QA defects, recovery risks and prioritized improvements, with optional implementation scoped separately.

## When should GIS workflows be automated?

Repetition alone is not sufficient: consider error rates, data arrival cadence, required reproducibility, available maintenance capacity and whether manual exceptions can be detected safely.
